Born from Speed

The Origin

In the heart of racing culture, where every millisecond counts and precision is everything, 2 Stroke was born. Our founder, a former motorsport engineer, brought the same obsessive attention to detail from the track to the distillery.

The name pays homage to the raw, unfiltered power of two-stroke engines — machines that deliver pure, uncompromising performance. Our vodka follows the same philosophy.

The Process

01

Selection

We source only the finest winter wheat from select farms. Each grain is tested for optimal starch content and purity.

02

Distillation

Triple distilled in custom copper pot stills designed by our founder. Each run is monitored with the precision of a Formula 1 pit crew.

03

Filtration

Filtered through activated charcoal and diamond dust. The result is crystal clarity and an impossibly smooth finish.

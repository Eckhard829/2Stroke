Serve Suggestions

From classic simplicity to bold creations. Discover the perfect way to enjoy 2 Stroke.

2 Stroke Original

The Ignition

A fiery start to any evening. The ginger beer adds a kick that complements the smoothness of 2 Stroke Original.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz 2 Stroke Original
  • 1 oz Fresh lime juice
  • 0.5 oz Agave nectar
  • Ginger beer to top
  • Lime wheel for garnish

Method

Shake vodka, lime juice, and agave with ice. Strain into a highball glass over fresh ice. Top with ginger beer. Garnish with a lime wheel.

2 Stroke Black

Black Smoke

Dark, mysterious, and impossibly smooth. The charcoal-filtered vodka meets espresso for a sophisticated after-dinner drink.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz 2 Stroke Black
  • 1 oz Coffee liqueur
  • 1 shot Fresh espresso (cooled)
  • 0.5 oz Simple syrup
  • Three coffee beans

Method

Shake all ingredients except coffee beans vigorously with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe glass. Garnish with three coffee beans.

2 Stroke Reserve

Reserve Sour

Classic sophistication meets oak-aged complexity. The egg white creates a silky texture that pairs beautifully with the vanilla notes.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz 2 Stroke Reserve
  • 1 oz Fresh lemon juice
  • 0.75 oz Simple syrup
  • 1 Egg white
  • Angostura bitters

Method

Dry shake all ingredients without ice. Add ice and shake again vigorously. Strain into a rocks glass. Dash bitters on top in a pattern.

2 Stroke Black

Midnight Mule

Our take on the classic Moscow Mule, elevated with the depth of 2 Stroke Black and a hint of activated charcoal.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz 2 Stroke Black
  • 0.75 oz Fresh lime juice
  • Ginger beer to top
  • Mint sprig
  • Lime wedge

Method

Build in a copper mug over crushed ice. Add vodka and lime juice. Top with ginger beer. Garnish with mint sprig and lime wedge.

2 Stroke Reserve

Ember Old Fashioned

A bold reimagining of the classic. The oak-aged Reserve stands up beautifully to the bitters and orange oils.

Ingredients

  • 2.5 oz 2 Stroke Reserve
  • 0.25 oz Rich simple syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 1 dash Orange bitters
  • Orange peel

Method

Stir all ingredients with ice for 30 seconds. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube. Express orange peel over drink and drop in.

2 Stroke Original

Pure & Simple

Sometimes perfection needs no embellishment. Experience 2 Stroke Original exactly as intended.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz 2 Stroke Original (chilled)
  • Lemon twist (optional)

Method

Pour chilled vodka into a frozen glass. Express lemon twist over the surface if desired. Sip and appreciate the purity.

Pro Tips

01

Chill Your Glass

Store your glasses in the freezer for at least 30 minutes before serving.

02

Quality Ice

Use large, clear ice cubes. They melt slower and dilute less.

03

Fresh Citrus

Always use freshly squeezed juice. Bottled simply cannot compare.
